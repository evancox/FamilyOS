Family Operating System

Small tools for running the family week

A visual weekly schedule kids can shape themselves, and a two-minute game that shows what a cluttered room actually costs the people looking for their shoes in it. Both are built for the ADHD-lifespan brain, and for households that would rather see their time than argue about it.

Family schedule
Drag-and-drop weekly planner. Unlimited people, per-person editable anchors, sub-tasks, tap-to-start block timers, and templates that stamp shared blocks across the whole household.

What the pile costs
Find five things in a messy room, then the same five in a clear one. The delta is what mess charges you for every search — every day. Plus the math on how many hours the house actually asks for.

What both tools have in common

  • Visibility over enforcement. Show the time; let people decide.
  • The clock ends you, not the task. No shame accounting.
  • Autonomy inside structure. Anchors are anchors; everything else is theirs.
  • Local-only. Everything lives in your browser — nothing leaves it.
